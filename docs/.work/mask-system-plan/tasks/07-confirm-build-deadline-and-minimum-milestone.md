# Confirm the build deadline and minimum milestone

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: none

## Question

What current event or demonstration date replaces the original 2026-10-17 target, and what must the minimum working Eye Display demonstrate by that date? When should optional additions stop so they do not delay that result?

## Context

- [Define control and event operation](03-define-control-and-event-operation.md) prioritizes getting the Eye Display working as quickly as possible and makes on-demand control optional.
- The original target had already passed when that priority was agreed on 2026-10-23. No replacement date has been agreed.

## Comments

### Deadline confirmed, 2026-10-26

Leo requires the Mask System to be usable and wearable by Friday 30 October 2026. Extra tweaks may happen between 30 and 31 October. This supersedes the unresolved replacement date in the earlier context. No time of day was specified.

The date is settled. This ticket remains open to confirm the minimum milestone and the cutoff for optional additions. The existing four-hour runtime, usable vision and accessible restart criteria have not been relaxed.

### Minimum milestone agreed, 2026-10-27

Leo agreed to a deadline of Friday 30 October 2026 at 18:00 Europe/Berlin. The minimum result is a wearable Mask System showing static X eyes, with manual brightness adjustment, four-hour runtime, usable vision, and pocket restart verified. The existing wearability and restart criteria still apply. Expression switching, animation, and the Voice System are optional.

The optional-feature cutoff remains to be clarified: Leo wrote "thursday 30", but Thursday is 29 October and Friday is 30 October. The proposed cutoff was Thursday 29 October at noon; do not treat that date or time as settled until clarified. Keep this ticket claimed pending that answer.

After 31 October, Leo is free to revise the minimum result and continue building without deadlines or complexity limitations. This is permission to revisit the Halloween minimum in later planning, not a requirement to add complexity or a revision of the current acceptance criteria.

### Cutoff clarified, 2026-10-27

Leo confirmed Thursday 29 October as the intended date, settling the proposed noon cutoff.

## Answer

Agreed with Leo on 2026-10-27.

- The Mask System must be usable and wearable by Friday 30 October 2026 at 18:00 Europe/Berlin.
- The minimum result is static X eyes, with manual brightness adjustment, four hours of runtime at intended brightness, usable vision, and pocket restart verified on the assembled Mask System. The existing criteria in [Define power, reliability, and safety criteria](04-define-power-reliability-and-safety-criteria.md) remain required, including X eyes within 10 seconds of initiating a pocket restart and the agreed wearability checks.
- Expression switching, animation, Pocket Controller, Phone Controller, Custom Visuals, and Voice System are optional additions. Stop optional feature work Thursday 29 October 2026 at 12:00 Europe/Berlin; reserve the remaining time before the deadline for assembly, runtime testing, and fixes.
- Extra tweaks may follow on 30–31 October once the minimum result passes. After 31 October, the minimum result may be revised and development may continue without deadlines or complexity limitations. The Halloween milestone does not permanently constrain the project.

The minimum result is agreed, not yet demonstrated. [Test donor glasses and select the Eye Display](09-test-donor-glasses-and-select-eye-display.md) supplies the next physical evidence needed for integration planning. A detailed assembly and verification sequence depends on those findings.
