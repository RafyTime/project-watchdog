# Define power, reliability, and safety criteria

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: none

## Question

What measurable runtime, restart, thermal, visibility, hearing, moisture, cable, battery, and field-service criteria must every proposed Mask System architecture satisfy?

## Answer

Agreed with Leo on 2026-10-23.

### Runtime and recovery

- Require four hours of operation at the intended event brightness. Verify runtime with the assembled Eye Display and the chosen power bank.
- Require a restart from the pocket power connection without removing the mask or opening the Mask System. X eyes must be visible again within 10 seconds of initiating the restart.

### Wearability

- Reject any layout that contacts the wearer's glasses, obstructs existing ventilation, or prevents navigation in dim event lighting.
- Verify glare, fogging, and comfort with the Eye Display operating. The existing mask's wear experience alone does not establish that the electronics layout passes.

### Event conditions and field service

- Plan for indoor use and sweat exposure. Remove the Mask System for rain.
- Preserve the existing two-handed removal movement when adding components and cables.
- A restart or battery swap should remain accessible during use. Repairs beyond those actions may require removing the mask.

### Follow-up validation

These are acceptance criteria, not evidence of a validated assembly. Select hardware and define its electrical, thermal, moisture, cable, and battery checks with the architecture. Detailed hearing and audio validation remains part of the optional Voice System work described in [Evaluate Voice System feasibility](05-evaluate-voice-system-feasibility.md).
