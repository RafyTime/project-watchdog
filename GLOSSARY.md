# Project Watchdog

Project Watchdog is a wearable electronic mask inspired by Wrench. This glossary names the parts and behaviors discussed throughout its design and build.

## Language

**Mask System**:
The complete wearable assembly, including the existing decorated mask and the electronic additions made for Project Watchdog.
_Avoid_: Cosplay, costume

**Eye Display**:
The paired visual area inside the mask's eyes that presents expressions and animations.
_Avoid_: Screen, goggles

**Expression**:
A named, reusable visual shown by the Eye Display, which may be static or animated.
_Avoid_: Face, emoji

**Pocket Controller**:
A small handheld control used to trigger Expressions without removing or touching the Mask System.
_Avoid_: Remote, button box

**Phone Controller**:
The phone-based interface used to trigger Expressions and, when supported, send Custom Visuals to the Mask System.
_Avoid_: App

**Custom Visual**:
An Eye Display design created or modified by the wearer outside the built-in Expression set.
_Avoid_: Custom expression, user graphic

**Voice System**:
The optional Mask System addition that captures, transforms, and plays the wearer's voice.
_Avoid_: Voice changer
